World content

The full-world bar is Voyage's own limit for each section (how much a world is allowed to hold), so 100 means that area is filled to the cap. Quests, arcs, narrative events and world voices have no limit of their own (Voyage accepted 6 MB of quests or events), so they score against an average area's share of this world's text (the world's total ÷ 12 areas). Only ready records count: filled in, and reachable in play. That means a quest a start or trigger offers, an event something starts, and an NPC with a description, personality, looks and a place.
